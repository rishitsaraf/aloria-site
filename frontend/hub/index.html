The Idea

Jewellery as a kit of parts

Every Aloria piece belongs to a component system. A primary stud, cuff or ring anchors the look; charms, jackets and chains build on top of it. Customers don't buy a design — they compose one. The result: the trendy, curated-stack look of multiple piercings and layered chains, without the piercings and without the price of fine jewellery.

Stackable

Components are engineered to combine — press-on, clip-on, thread-on. Each piece multiplies the looks of every other piece.

Customisable

Four stone colors, five cuts, two platings across the range. The same stack, in your palette.

Accessible

925 silver base, premium CZ stones, gold & rhodium plating. Luxury-inspired, everyday-priced.
